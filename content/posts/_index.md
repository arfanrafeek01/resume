Welcome to the blog index of an accountant. 

If you are expecting thrilling bedtime stories, you might want to look away—unless your idea of high-octane drama involves tracking down a £0.02 reconciliation variance at 6 PM on a Friday.

Here you’ll find field notes, battle scars, and practical write-ups on:

- **The Real Numbers:** Surviving VAT returns, utility disputes, and franchise reconciliations without losing sanity.
- **Tools of the Trade:** Pushing Excel, Xero, and POS systems past their factory defaults to save manual labour.
- **Operations & Logic:** Bridging the gap between the chaotic trading floor and the clean, orderly general ledger.

Feel free to browse around. Everything balances here.
